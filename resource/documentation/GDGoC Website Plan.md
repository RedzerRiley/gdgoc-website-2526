**II. Project Timeline** 

The development of the GDGoC Mapúa Website will follow an iterative development approach consisting of planning, design, implementation, review, security validation, and deployment. The project will be divided into phases to ensure that each feature is properly developed, reviewed, and integrated into the website.

| Phase | Timeline | Activities |
| :---- | :---- | ----- |
| **Phase 1 – Planning and Design** | **Week 1** | Review project requirements, finalize feature specifications, establish development tasks, and align the website with the “Legacy Made Timeless” theme**.** |
| **Phase 2 – UI/UX Improvements** | **Week 2** | Implement the Hero Section and Initial Landing Banner, improve Color Contrast and Readability for Cards, resolve Responsive Container Layouts and Breakpoints, and implement lightweight Micro-interactions and Hover States. |
| **Phase 3 – Core Website Features** | **Week 3** | Develop the Footer with organizational information and links, implement Account Profile Customization, and begin development of the Freedom Wall Page. |
| **Phase 4 – Community Features** | **Week 4** | Complete the Freedom Wall Page, implement GDGoC Mapúa-themed Stickers, develop Previous Events Quotes, and implement Profile Interactability of Members. |
| **Phase 5 – Content and Documentation** | **Week 5** | Implement About Us Page Officer Quotes, complete the website-wide “Legacy Made Timeless” design alignment, and prepare the project Documentation File. |
| **Phase 6 – Review, Security, and Deployment** | **Week 6** | Conduct code review, perform security checks, resolve identified issues, conduct final testing and review, merge approved changes into the production/main branch, and finalize project documentation. |

**III. Proposed Plan**

The proposed plan focuses on improving the functionality, usability, accessibility, community interaction, and visual identity of the GDGoC Mapúa Website.

### **1\. \[fix\] Responsive Container Layouts & Breakpoints**

Audit and resolve container overflow, alignment, and spacing issues across small viewports, particularly on mobile and tablet devices. The objective is to ensure that website content remains properly positioned, readable, and accessible across different screen sizes.

### **2\. \[ui/ux\] Color Contrast & Readability for Cards**

Improve the text contrast on goal cards and containers to meet WCAG readability standards. This will improve accessibility and make important information easier to read across different display conditions.

### **3\. \[feat\] Hero Section & Initial Landing Banner**

Add a dedicated Hero section at the top of the homepage before the About Us section. The Hero section will immediately introduce the GDGoC Mapúa chapter, communicate its identity, and provide a stronger visual introduction to visitors.

### **4\. \[feat\] Lightweight Micro-interactions & Hover States**

Implement subtle CSS animations and hover states on interactive components to improve the overall user experience. Animations will remain lightweight to maintain website responsiveness and avoid unnecessary performance overhead.

### **5\. \[feat\] Footer w/ Information**

Develop a website footer containing relevant organizational information such as contact details, address, and links to different pages of the website. This will provide users with easier access to important information and site navigation.

### **6\. \[feat\] “Freedom Wall” Page**

Develop a Freedom Wall page where GDGoC Mapúa members can post messages and content related to the organization. Members may customize their messages using different background colors, GIFs, pictures, and categories. They may also choose whether to remain anonymous.

All submitted content must comply with the GDGoC Mapúa Community Guidelines. Once submitted, messages will undergo review by officers before being displayed publicly on the website.

### **7\. \[feat\] “Freedom Wall” GDGoC Mapúa-themed Stickers**

Add GDGoC Mapúa-themed stickers that members can use to customize and express themselves through their Freedom Wall messages.

### **8\. \[feat\] Documentation File**

Create a Markdown documentation file within the repository containing detailed information about the website, including its features, setup instructions, usage, and project structure. This will assist current and future developers in maintaining and extending the project.

### **9\. \[feat\] Account Profile Customization**

Allow GDGoC Mapúa members to customize their account profiles by adding a profile picture, profile description, and links through which other users may reach them, subject to the GDGoC Mapúa Community Guidelines.

Members may also choose from a predefined set of profile pictures and descriptions.

### **10\. \[feat\] Previous Events Quotes**

Add functionality to the Previous Events section that allows members to submit thoughts or feedback about an event. Approved submissions will be displayed as quotes when users access the corresponding event.

All submitted messages will be reviewed by officers to ensure that their content follows the GDGoC Mapúa Community Guidelines before being displayed.

### **11\. \[feat\] About Us Page Officer Quotes**

Allow officers to add personal quotes to the About Us and Meet the Team sections. Officers may also personalize their quotes using emojis to provide a more engaging and personable presentation.

### **12\. \[feat\] Profile Interactability of Members**

Allow users to view other members' profiles by clicking their profile picture or name. This functionality will be available through the Freedom Wall, About Us, and Member Quotes sections of the Events page.

### **13\. \[feat\] Align Website Design with “Legacy Made Timeless”**

Customize the design of all website pages according to the tenure's theme, “Legacy Made Timeless.” This will ensure that the visual identity, layouts, styling, and overall presentation of the website are consistent with the chapter's theme.
